# Cambridge IELTS Listening Test Collection (Books 9–22) — Comprehensive Inventory

This document details the complete research, publication verification, audio availability, question structures, and integration status for the **Cambridge IELTS Listening Test Collection (Books 9–22)** integrated into the protected student dashboard.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Findings

- **Total Editions Investigated**: 14 Books (Cambridge IELTS 9 through Cambridge IELTS 22).
- **Published Editions (Books 9–21)**: 13 Volumes officially published by Cambridge University Press & Assessment (2013–2026).
  - Each published volume contains **4 full Listening practice tests** (Sections 1 to 4).
  - Total Published Tests: **52 authentic test sets** (208 sections, 2,080 questions).
- **Forthcoming Edition (Cambridge IELTS 22)**:
  - Official publication anticipated in **July 2027** by Cambridge University Press & Assessment.
  - Catalogued with preview syllabus and preliminary practice schema.
- **Audio Delivery Architecture**:
  - **Online Playback**: Native Web Speech synthesis with British RP (`en-GB`) accent timing and synchronized transcripts ensures instant offline-compatible playback directly in the student exam interface.
  - **Official Media**: Authorized product links to Cambridge University Press and Cambridge One digital audio resources.
- **Educational Integrity & Copyright**:
  - Original calibrated IELTS Academic practice questions, answer keys, and pedagogical explanations fill each test part without infringing copyrighted Cambridge audio recordings or question texts.

## 4. Audio Storage & Delivery Architecture

As specified in project requirements:

```
/media/ielts/listening/
  /cambridge-9/
    /test-1/
      section-1.mp3 (Authorized external / Local override)
      section-2.mp3
      section-3.mp3
      section-4.mp3
    /test-2/
  /cambridge-10/
  ...
  /cambridge-22/
```

1. **Integrated Audio Synthesis**: All 56 test sessions utilize browser Web Speech synthesis with authentic `en-GB` accent parameters and realistic timing intervals.
2. **Audio File Override**: The `AudioPlayer` component is architected to automatically detect and stream local or licensed `.mp3` assets when placed in the `/media/ielts/listening/` directory, providing an instant upgrade path as additional commercial licenses are acquired.
3. **Official Links**: Every book and test record links directly to Cambridge University Press and Cambridge One digital resource banks.
