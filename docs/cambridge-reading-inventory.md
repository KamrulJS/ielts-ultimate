### Academic Reading Test Architecture

Each authentic Cambridge Academic Reading test consists of:
- **Total Duration:** Exactly 60 minutes (strict countdown timer).
- **Total Questions:** 40 questions distributed across 3 passages:
  - **Passage 1:** ~750–900 words, descriptive or factual text (Questions 1–13 or 1–14).
  - **Passage 2:** ~850–1,000 words, analytical or discursive text (Questions 14–26 or 15–27).
  - **Passage 3:** ~950–1,150 words, complex argument or theoretical discussion (Questions 27–40 or 28–40).
- **No Extra Transfer Time:** Unlike the IELTS Listening module (which gives 10 minutes for paper-based or 2 minutes for computer-delivered review), Reading requires all answers to be recorded directly within the 60 minutes.
